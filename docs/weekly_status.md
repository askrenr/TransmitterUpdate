## Notes

State/province percentages reflect the **current location of each transmitter**, its most recent GPS fix in the 30-day window, rather than the total number of GPS fixes collected. Migration status compares each transmitter's first and last fix in the window: **Migrating** is 150+ km with at least 1° of latitude change in either direction, **Transitional** is 60+ km without that, and **Local** is everything else. 12 transmitters are classed as migrating (12 south), 0 transitional and 20 local.
